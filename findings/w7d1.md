## Week 7 Day 1 — Experimental Findings and Interim Model Selection

### 1. KNN Imputation Investigation

KNN imputation was investigated as an alternative to median imputation. Due to computational limitations, the comparison used 5,000 training and 2,000 validation observations.

Median imputation achieved an accuracy of 0.5755, macro F1 of 0.5170 and weighted F1 of 0.5818. KNN imputation achieved 0.5675, 0.5013 and 0.5745, respectively.

**Finding:** KNN did not demonstrate an overall improvement. Median imputation was retained. This comparison is exploratory and does not establish full-dataset superiority.

### 2. Test-Set Model Evaluation

Three selected models were evaluated on 206,200 chronologically held-out observations.

| Model | Accuracy | Macro F1 | Weighted F1 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Previous Random Forest | 0.6352 | 0.5654 | 0.6373 |
| Tuned Random Forest | 0.6210 | 0.5777 | 0.6236 |
| Gradient Boosting | 0.6154 | 0.5731 | 0.6191 |

**Finding:** Tuned Random Forest achieved the highest macro F1, while Previous Random Forest achieved the highest accuracy and weighted F1.

### 3. Per-Class Performance and Error Analysis

The Tuned Random Forest achieved the following test recall:

- Good: 0.8025
- Satisfactory: 0.6566
- Moderate: 0.5658
- Poor: 0.4454
- Very Poor: 0.4872
- Severe: 0.6641

Its confusion matrix showed substantial confusion between neighbouring AQI categories, particularly Satisfactory, Moderate and Poor.

Error severity analysis identified:
- Correct predictions: 128,049
- Adjacent-category errors: 71,435
- Errors two or more categories away: 6,716

Adjacent-category errors represented 91.41% of all incorrect predictions.

### 4. Interim Model Selection

Tuned Random Forest with median imputation was selected as the primary model candidate because it achieved the highest test macro F1 (0.5777), aligning with the project's objective of balanced classification across six AQI categories.

Its configuration:

- `n_estimators=200`
- `max_depth=10`
- `min_samples_leaf=5`
- `min_samples_split=10`
- `max_features="sqrt"`
- `class_weight="balanced"`
- `random_state=42`

Previous Random Forest remains an alternative when accuracy is prioritized.

### 5. Limitations

- KNN was evaluated on a reduced sample rather than the full dataset.
- Poor and Very Poor categories remain difficult to classify.
- The test period was used in earlier experiments and is not an untouched independent holdout.
- Test performance alone does not establish geographical transferability or deployment readiness.
